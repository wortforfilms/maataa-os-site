What we collect

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no forms. The pages are plain HTML with no scripts. Fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties.
  • Hosting logs. The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for, to deliver the page and protect it from attacks. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

  • The RSS feed is a file you download; we cannot see who subscribes.
